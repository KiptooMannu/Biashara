BIASHARA

Helping small businesses run better with one connected system.

MSMEs are the engine of most economies, yet they are often forced to manage sales, stock, customers, payroll and cash flow across disconnected tools. BIASHARA brings those operations into one platform and adds an assistant that tells the owner what is happening and what to do next.

Main problem

Small businesses are operating in a gap that existing tools do not fill.

Fragmented tools

Stock, sales, finance and customers are commonly split across spreadsheets and multiple apps.

Slow decisions

Owners get reports after the fact, but not the insight needed to act in real time.

Hidden losses

Stockouts, late collections and weak inventory decisions quietly erode margin and cash flow.

What BIASHARA does

One platform for everyday operations.

Core modules

  • Inventory and stock movement
  • POS and sales workflow
  • CRM and customer tracking
  • Supplier and procurement management

Business control

  • Finance, expenses and invoicing
  • HR, payroll and leave
  • Audit trail and role-based access
  • Dashboards and business health insights
Why it matters

It turns operations from reactive to proactive.

Instead of asking “what happened?”, the business can ask “why is it happening, what is costing us, and what should we do next?”

Differentiator

It classifies the user’s question, runs real queries, and explains the numbers it used. That makes the advice auditable and useful, rather than generic or invented.
