BIASHARA

Helping small businesses run better with one connected system.

Most MSMEs are stuck using scattered tools for sales, stock, accounting and customers. The result is slow decisions, hidden losses and too much manual work.

90% of businesses 50%+ of jobs Trillions financing gap World Bank source

These figures come from World Bank SME finance research: SMEs make up around 90% of all businesses and account for more than half of global employment.

The main problem

MSMEs need software that is affordable, practical and built for daily operations.

Fragmented tools

SMEs often manage stock, sales, cash flow and customers across disconnected tools or spreadsheets, making coordination harder than it should be.

Slow decisions

When owners cannot see the full picture quickly, growth slows and everyday decisions around stock, hiring and spending become reactive.

Hidden losses

In emerging markets and developing economies, the financing gap leaves many businesses unable to invest, expand or protect margins when disruptions hit.

The solution

One platform for everyday business operations.

What it covers

  • Inventory and stock movement
  • POS and sales workflow
  • CRM and customer tracking
  • Procurement and suppliers

Why it stands out

  • Finance, payroll and reporting in one place
  • Role-based access and audit controls
  • AI assistant that explains the numbers with evidence
How it works

From a transaction to a decision in one flow.

Capture

A sale, purchase, expense or customer interaction is recorded once and immediately connected to the rest of the system.

Connect

Inventory, cash flow, customer activity and reporting are updated together so the business sees a consistent picture.

Act

The owner can respond faster with evidence-backed guidance instead of waiting for manual analysis and spreadsheets.

Why it matters

It turns operations from reactive to proactive.

Instead of asking “what happened?”, the business can ask “why is it happening, what is costing us, and what should we do next?”

That makes BIASHARA relevant for retail, wholesale and growing SMEs that need better control without enterprise complexity.
